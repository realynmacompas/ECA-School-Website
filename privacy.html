Privacy Policy

Elim Christian Academy website · Last updated October 22, 2026

Visitors to this website

The public pages of this website don't ask you to sign in, and they don't collect personal information from you. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Staff who sign in to the admin area

Only school staff approved by the website administrator can sign in, using their Google account. When they do, we receive their name, email address and profile picture from Google. We use this only to check that they're on the approved staff list and to show who is signed in. A sign-in cookie keeps them signed in for a few hours.

Google Drive

The school can connect a Google Drive account so that photos uploaded to this website are also saved there as a backup. The website can only see and change the folders and files it creates itself. It can't open any other files in that Drive. The school can disconnect Drive at any time from the admin settings, or from the Google account's security settings.

Photos and posts

News, events, announcements and photo albums are published by school staff. If you'd like a photo of you or your child removed, please contact the school and we'll take it down.

Sharing

We don't sell or share personal information. Information is stored with the services that run this website (Vercel for hosting, a MySQL database, and Google for sign-in and Drive).

Contact

For questions about this policy or to ask for something to be removed, please contact Elim Christian Academy through the details on the home page.
