  1. In Meta for Developers, add the school's Page admin to the app (App roles > Add people).

  3. They log in to Facebook and pick the school's Page.

Posts made earlier stay on the old Page. Only posts published with "Also post to the school's Facebook page" ticked are sent, once each.
