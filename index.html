Why choose ECA

A School That Feels Like Family

For more than three decades, Elim Christian Academy has helped students in Trece Martires grow in knowledge and in faith. Our teachers work closely with students and parents to help every learner grow in character, faith, and academics.

“Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it.”

Proverbs 22:6
  • Christ-Centered Learning

    Bible and values education are part of every school day.

  • Dedicated Teachers

    Teachers who guide students in and out of the classroom.

  • Safe Campus

    An orderly, respectful environment where students can focus on learning.

  • Well-Rounded Growth

    Sports, arts, and school events that build confidence.

Basic Requirements

  • PSA Birth Certificate (photocopy)
  • Report Card (SF9)
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • Two (2) recent 2x2 ID pictures
  • Learner Reference Number (LRN)

Transferees may need additional documents. Please confirm with the registrar.
